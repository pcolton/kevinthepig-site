Support

Kevin the Pig is a little toy app for iPhone and iPad. If something isn't working, or you just want to say hi, email apps@colton.net — usually answered within a few days.

How to play

Poke
Tap Kevin. Tap quickly for a flurry of boops.
Squeeze
Pinch him with two fingers. Squeeze harder for a bigger squeak.
Carry
Drag him anywhere. Move left and right to turn him; push down to squash him into the paper.
Throw
Flick and let go. Toss him up, sideways, or slam him down.
Spin
Swipe on the paper beside him to spin him like a turntable.
New edition
Double-tap the paper to roll out the next colorway.
Dizzy
Shake your device, or spin him really fast.
Portrait
Tap the camera button, drag inside the print to frame your shot, then Save to Photos. The photo develops like instant film.

Troubleshooting

  • Saving a photo fails. Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Kevin and allow Add Photos Only.
  • No sound. Check the Ring/Silent switch or Action button and your volume.
  • Saving takes a few seconds. That's expected: each photo is carefully rendered with realistic studio lighting.

Beta feedback

Testing the beta? The quickest way to report a problem is to take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open TestFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback. Email works too.
