Support

Kevin the Pig is a squeaky fidget toy for iPhone, made for iPhone Duo too. If something isn't working, or you just want to say hi, email apps@colton.net — usually answered within a few days.

How to play

Poke
Tap him anywhere.
Squeeze
Pinch him with two fingers. Squeeze harder for a bigger squeak.
Toss
Drag him and let go.
Spin
Swipe across the paper. Spin him fast enough and he gets dizzy.
Surprise
Shake your iPhone: bacon, gold coins or glowing confetti, depending on his material.
Next colour
Double-tap the paper.
Shade and finish
The sliders under the colours and materials.
Take a photo
Tap the camera; drag to turn or tilt, pinch to zoom, then Save to Photos. The print develops like instant film.
Freeze a moment
Tap the camera mid-squeeze, spin or shake.
iPhone Duo
Open it flat or stand it up like a book and he roams the whole spread; close it and he pops out onto the cover display.

Troubleshooting

  • Saving a photo fails. Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Kevin and allow Add Photos Only.
  • No sound. Check the Ring/Silent switch or Action button and your volume.
  • Kevin Plus on a new device. Open any Plus item and tap Restore purchase.
