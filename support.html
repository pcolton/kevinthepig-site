Support

Kevin the Pig is a squeaky fidget toy for iPhone, iPad and Mac. If something isn't working, or you just want to say hi, email hi@kevinthepig.com. Replies usually come within a few days.

How to play

Poke
Tap him anywhere.
Squeeze
Pinch him with two fingers. Squeeze harder for a bigger squeak.
Toss
Drag him and let go.
Spin
Swipe across the paper. Spin him fast enough and he gets dizzy.
Surprise
Shake your device to see what tumbles out. Each themed stage has its own treats.
Next colour
Double-tap the paper.
Shade and finish
The sliders under the colours and materials.
Take a photo
Tap the camera; drag to turn or tilt, pinch to zoom, then Save to Photos. The print develops like instant film.
Freeze a moment
Tap the camera mid-squeeze, spin or shake.
iPhone Duo
Open it flat or stand it up like a book and he roams the whole spread; close it and he pops out onto the cover display.
Mac
Click Kevin to poke him, drag and release to toss him, or pinch over him with a trackpad to squeeze. Choose Play → Shake for a surprise.

Themes and outfits

Open the hanger to pick a theme in Looks or change his accessories in Dress Kevin. To keep a combination, choose Save current look and give it a name.

Wild West and its cowboy outfit are free. Space Cadet, Under the Sea, Fun in the Sun and Rainy Day come with Kevin Plus.

Troubleshooting

  • Saving a photo fails. Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Kevin and allow Add Photos Only.
  • No sound. Check the Ring/Silent switch or Action button and your volume.
  • Kevin Plus on a new device. Open any Plus item and tap Restore purchase.
