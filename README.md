# Kevin the Pig — site

Public pages for the Kevin the Pig iOS app, served by GitHub Pages at https://kevinthepig.com:

- `index.html` — landing page
- `privacy.html` — privacy policy
- `support.html` — support and how to play
- `terms.html` — terms of use
- `app/` — kevinthepig.com/app, the link shared prints carry: the App Store once Kevin is on it, the site until then
- `store.js` — checks Apple's lookup service; shows the App Store badge (and sends /app to the App Store) only once Kevin is released
- `media/` — screenshots and videos from the app's `marketing/app-store/1.0/`, sized for the web, and Apple's App Store badges (toolbox.marketingtools.apple.com, unmodified)

Colors and icon come from the app: the Strawberry Milk edition (light) and Midnight (dark).
