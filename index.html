A real squeaky toy

Scanned from a real rubber pig

Kevin was 3D-scanned from an actual squeaky toy, and every squeak and oink you hear is a recording of the real thing.

Squeeze him and he squashes. Pinch him and his belly puffs up. Spin him until he's dizzy, or shake your iPhone for a surprise: bacon, gold coins or glowing confetti.
