Scanned from a real toy pig

Kevin was 3D-scanned from an actual squeaky toy, and every squeak and oink you hear is a recording of the real thing.

Now on Mac

Kevin has a resizable window on your Mac. Click to poke him, drag to toss him, or pinch on your trackpad to make him squeak. Choose Play → Shake to see what tumbles out.
