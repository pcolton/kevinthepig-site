Kevin the Pig

A squishy vinyl pig who lives on a sheet of studio paper. Poke him, squeeze him, toss him across the room — then take his portrait.

Poke & squeeze

Tap to boop, pinch with two fingers to squish. He squeaks.

Toss him around

Pick him up, turn him, fling him — or push him flat into the paper.

Portrait studio

Frame a shot and watch it develop like instant film, then save it to Photos.

Five editions

Double-tap the paper to roll out a new colorway: Strawberry Milk to Midnight.
